How we make money

PixPlay Learning is free to read. We keep it running in three ways:

  • Affiliate links. Some tool links may be affiliate links. If you sign up or buy through one, the company pays us a commission. You pay the same price.
  • Our own products. We sell ebooks, templates and the Strategic Script System on Payhip.
  • Services. Some businesses hire PixPlaySG to make their content.

A commission never decides what we recommend. We only mark a tool "We use this" if we really use it, and we say who a tool is not for, too.

We don't promise income. Results depend on your topic, your effort and time, and many people earn little in their first year. Anyone promising fast passive income is selling you something.
