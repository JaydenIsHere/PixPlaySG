What it is

Metricool is a social media planner and analytics tool. You connect accounts like Threads, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and LinkedIn, then schedule posts from one calendar. It also shows when your audience is online and how each post performed.

How to use it in your work

  • Write a week of posts on Sunday and schedule them all at once.
  • Post the same announcement to several platforms with small changes for each.
  • Check which posts and times work best, and plan next week around them.
  • Freelancers: manage clients' accounts and export simple reports.

Who it's for

Content creatorsSmall business ownersMarketersFreelancers

How it helps your content

  • Consistency without posting by hand every day.
  • Suggested best times based on your own audience.
  • See which topics and formats get the most views and replies.

Good to know

⚠️
  • The free plan limits how much you can schedule and some features. Check the current limits.
  • Scheduling saves time, but reply to comments yourself. Conversation matters.
  • Some networks or features may require a paid plan.

Pricing

Free plan with limits; paid plans for more brands, posts and features. Plans change, so check the official pricing page.

Quick start

  1. Sign up and create a "brand" for your business or account.
  2. Connect Threads, Instagram or other profiles.
  3. Open the planning calendar, create a post, pick a time and schedule it.
