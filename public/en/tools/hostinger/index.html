What it is

Hostinger is a web hosting company. You can host a WordPress site or blog, or use its website builder (with AI help) to make a site without code. Plans can include a domain name and business email.

How to use it in your work

  • A website for your business with your services, prices and contact details.
  • A WordPress blog to publish guides that people find on Google.
  • A professional email address on your own domain.

Who it's for

Small business ownersFreelancersContent creators

How it helps your content

  • A home base you own, not just social profiles.
  • Articles that can bring visitors from Google for months.
  • A place to put your links, products and sign-up forms.

Good to know

⚠️
  • No free plan. Introductory prices are usually for the first term, so check renewal prices.
  • WordPress gives flexibility but needs some upkeep (updates, backups).
  • Choose a plan for your actual needs. Most small sites don't need the biggest one.

Pricing

Paid plans (no free plan). Compare the first-term price with the renewal price. Plans change, so check the official pricing page.

Quick start

  1. Pick a plan and a domain name.
  2. Choose WordPress or the website builder.
  3. Add your key pages (home, about, services, contact) and publish.
