What it is

HeyGen creates videos from a script using AI avatars, either stock presenters or a digital version of yourself (with your consent). It can also translate an existing video into other languages with matching lip movements.

How to use it in your work

  • Office workers: turn a training document or update into a short explainer video.
  • Translate a product or course video into Chinese, English and more.
  • Make the same video in several versions without re-filming.

Who it's for

Office workersMarketersCoaches & educatorsSmall business owners

How it helps your content

  • Video content without a camera or studio.
  • Reach new language markets with one original video.
  • Update a video by editing the script instead of re-recording.

Good to know

⚠️
  • Only create avatars or voice clones of people who have given permission.
  • Be transparent when a video uses an AI avatar where it matters to viewers.
  • The free plan is limited. Check the current plan and usage rules.

Pricing

Free plan with limited videos; paid plans for more. Plans change, so check the official pricing page.

Quick start

  1. Sign up and pick a stock avatar to try it.
  2. Paste your script and choose a voice and language.
  3. Generate, review, and edit the script until it sounds natural.
