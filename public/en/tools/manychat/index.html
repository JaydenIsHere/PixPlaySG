What it is

ManyChat automates conversations in social messaging. The classic use: someone comments a keyword on your post, and they automatically get a DM with your link. You can also build question-and-answer flows, collect emails and book appointments inside the chat. It does not work on Threads.

How to use it in your work

  • Comment "GUIDE" → get the free download link in DMs.
  • Answer common questions (price, opening hours, location) any time of day.
  • Collect a name and email inside the chat before sending a freebie.
  • Let people book a call or appointment from a DM.

Who it's for

Content creatorsSmall business ownersCoaches & educatorsMarketers

How it helps your content

  • Turn a post into a conversation, and comments help a post reach more people.
  • Deliver lead magnets instantly instead of replying by hand.
  • See which posts actually start conversations.

Good to know

⚠️
  • No Threads support. It's for Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, TikTok and a few others.
  • The free plan is limited; plan on Pro if DMs are central to your business.
  • Write replies like a person, and follow each platform's messaging rules.

Pricing

Limited free plan; most useful features are on the paid Pro plan. Plans change, so check the official pricing page.

Quick start

  1. Sign up and connect your Instagram (or other) account.
  2. Choose the "comment keyword → DM" template.
  3. Write a friendly reply with your link, test it from a friend's account, then go live.
