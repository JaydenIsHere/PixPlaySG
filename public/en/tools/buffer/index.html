What it is

Buffer is a social media scheduler known for being easy to use. You set up a posting schedule (time slots), then add posts to a queue and Buffer publishes them. It supports major platforms including Threads, Instagram, LinkedIn and Facebook, and has an AI assistant to help rewrite posts.

How to use it in your work

  • Set fixed posting times, then just keep the queue filled.
  • Rewrite one post into versions for different platforms with the AI assistant.
  • Small teams: draft posts and get them approved before they go out (paid plans).

Who it's for

Small business ownersContent creatorsFreelancers

How it helps your content

  • A steady posting rhythm with very little setup.
  • Quick variations of a post for each platform.
  • Basic stats to see what's working.

Good to know

⚠️
  • The free plan covers only a few channels. Check the current limits.
  • Deeper analytics and team features are on paid plans.
  • Simpler than all-in-one tools, which is great to start but limited for agencies.

Pricing

Free plan for a few channels; paid plans for more channels and features. Plans change, so check the official pricing page.

Quick start

  1. Sign up and connect up to your free plan's channel limit.
  2. Set your posting time slots.
  3. Add posts to the queue and let Buffer publish them.
