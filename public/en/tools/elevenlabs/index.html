What it is

ElevenLabs turns text into speech that sounds close to a real person, in many languages and voices. You can also dub audio into other languages, or create a digital copy of your own voice to narrate content.

How to use it in your work

  • Voiceovers for short videos, product demos or tutorials.
  • Narrate presentations or training materials without recording yourself.
  • Turn blog posts or lessons into audio versions.

Who it's for

Content creatorsCoaches & educatorsMarketersOffice workers

How it helps your content

  • Professional-sounding narration without a microphone or studio.
  • Content in several languages from the same script.
  • Fix a line by editing text instead of re-recording.

Good to know

⚠️
  • Only clone voices you have the right to use, like your own.
  • Check whether your plan allows commercial use before using it in paid or business content.
  • Listen through the whole output. AI can mispronounce names and mixed-language words.

Pricing

Free plan with a monthly limit; check which plans allow commercial use. Plans change, so check the official pricing page.

Quick start

  1. Sign up and open the text-to-speech tool.
  2. Paste a short script and try a few voices.
  3. Adjust the settings, download the audio and add it to your video.
