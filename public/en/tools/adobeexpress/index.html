What it is

Adobe Express is an easy design tool from Adobe. Start from thousands of templates to make social posts, flyers, presentations and short videos. It includes one-click tools like background removal and resizing, plus generative AI (Adobe Firefly) for images and text effects.

How to use it in your work

  • Office workers: quick event flyers, internal announcements or simple slides.
  • Small businesses: menu boards, promotions and social posts in your brand colours.
  • Resize one design for Instagram, Threads, Facebook and stories in a click.

Who it's for

Office workersSmall business ownersContent creatorsCoaches & educators

How it helps your content

  • Professional-looking visuals without design skills.
  • A brand kit (logo, colours, fonts) keeps every post consistent.
  • Generate images or text effects when you don't have a photo.

Good to know

⚠️
  • Some templates, assets and AI credits are Premium only.
  • Similar to Canva. If you already use Canva, compare before switching.
  • Check usage rights for AI images in commercial work.

Pricing

Free plan; Premium unlocks more templates, assets and AI credits. Plans change, so check the official pricing page.

Quick start

  1. Sign up for free and choose what you're making (post, flyer, video).
  2. Pick a template and swap in your text and photos.
  3. Resize for each platform and download or schedule.
