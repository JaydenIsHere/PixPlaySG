What it is

Systeme.io puts several tools in one place: landing pages and sales funnels, email marketing and automation, online course hosting, a simple website or blog, and checkout pages to take payments. It's designed for solo business owners who don't want to connect many separate tools.

How to use it in your work

  • A landing page for a free guide, plus an automatic welcome email.
  • A sales page and checkout for a course or coaching package.
  • Host an online course with lessons for your students.

Who it's for

Coaches & educatorsContent creatorsSmall business ownersFreelancers

How it helps your content

  • Lead-magnet pages and follow-up emails in one tool.
  • Turn your know-how into a course without technical setup.
  • Fewer tools to pay for and learn.

Good to know

⚠️
  • All-in-one means each part is simpler than a specialist tool.
  • Design options are more limited than a dedicated website builder.
  • The free plan limits contacts and some features. Check current limits.

Pricing

Free plan with limits on contacts and features; paid plans for more. Plans change, so check the official pricing page.

Quick start

  1. Sign up for free and pick a funnel template.
  2. Edit the page text and connect an email for sign-ups.
  3. Publish, then test the sign-up flow yourself.
