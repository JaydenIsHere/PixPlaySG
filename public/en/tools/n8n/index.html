What it is

n8n is a workflow automation tool, similar to Make, that's popular for AI workflows. You connect nodes on a canvas to move data between apps and add AI steps such as summarising, classifying or drafting text. You can use n8n Cloud, or install the free community edition on your own server.

How to use it in your work

  • Incoming emails → summarised by AI and posted to your team chat.
  • New articles from websites you follow → collected and drafted into post ideas by AI.
  • Support requests → sorted by topic and sent to the right person.
  • Data synced between your CRM, spreadsheets and email tool.

Who it's for

Tech-comfortable usersOffice workersMarketersFreelancers

How it helps your content

  • Build a research → draft pipeline: collect sources, let AI write a first draft for you to edit.
  • Automatically tag and organise content ideas by topic.
  • Repurpose a long post into shorter versions for different platforms.

Good to know

⚠️
  • More technical than Make. Comfortable with a bit of setup? You'll be fine.
  • Self-hosting is free but you look after the server, updates and backups yourself.
  • Review AI outputs before they go anywhere public.

Pricing

Free if you host it yourself; n8n Cloud is paid, with a free trial. Plans change, so check the official pricing page.

Quick start

  1. Start a n8n Cloud trial (easiest) or install the community edition.
  2. Import a template from the n8n template library.
  3. Connect your accounts, test each step, then activate.
