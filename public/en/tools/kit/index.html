What it is

Kit (formerly ConvertKit) is an email tool made for creators. You make sign-up forms and landing pages, collect subscribers, and send newsletters. Paid plans add automated email sequences, like a welcome series that sends itself.

How to use it in your work

  • A sign-up page for your free download or newsletter.
  • A weekly email with your latest posts, tips or offers.
  • Tag subscribers by interest so you send them relevant emails.

Who it's for

Content creatorsCoaches & educatorsSmall business ownersFreelancers

How it helps your content

  • Own your audience. Emails reach people even when algorithms change.
  • Send every new piece of content straight to readers who asked for it.
  • Learn what your audience wants from replies and clicks.

Good to know

⚠️
  • Automations and sequences are on paid plans.
  • Free plans show Kit branding.
  • Follow email rules: only email people who signed up, and always include unsubscribe.

Pricing

Free plan for a large number of subscribers; automations need a paid plan. Check current limits. Plans change, so check the official pricing page.

Quick start

  1. Sign up for free and create a landing page or form.
  2. Share the link in your bio and posts.
  3. Write a short welcome email and send your first newsletter.
