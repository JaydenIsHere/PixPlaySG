What it is

Captions is an AI video app built for people who talk to the camera. It adds animated captions automatically, can trim silences and filler words, and offers AI tools such as translating your video into other languages.

How to use it in your work

  • Film a tip on your phone → captions and clean cuts in minutes.
  • Remove long pauses and "um"s from a talking video automatically.
  • Make a version of your video in another language for a new audience.

Who it's for

Content creatorsSmall business ownersCoaches & educators

How it helps your content

  • Faster editing for daily talking-head videos.
  • Eye-catching captions that keep people watching.
  • Reach audiences in other languages.

Good to know

⚠️
  • Most features are on paid plans. Check before you build a routine around it.
  • Always proofread captions and translations.
  • AI-translated or AI-altered video should be used honestly; don't mislead viewers.

Pricing

Free trial; most features need a subscription. Check the current plan. Plans change, so check the official pricing page.

Quick start

  1. Download the app or use the web version and start a trial.
  2. Record or import a talking video.
  3. Let it add captions and cuts, review everything, then export.
