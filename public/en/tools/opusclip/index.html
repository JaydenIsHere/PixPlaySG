What it is

Opus Clip uses AI to find the most interesting moments in a long video, such as a podcast, webinar, livestream or YouTube video, and cuts them into short vertical clips. It adds captions and reframes the shot so the speaker stays in the middle.

How to use it in your work

  • A 1-hour webinar → 10 short clips to post over two weeks.
  • A podcast episode → highlight clips with captions.
  • A product demo → short vertical teasers for social media.

Who it's for

Content creatorsCoaches & educatorsMarketersSmall business owners

How it helps your content

  • Many posts from one recording, so you film less and post more.
  • Captions help people watch without sound.
  • Vertical reframing saves manual editing.

Good to know

⚠️
  • AI picks aren't always the best moments. Review and adjust the clips.
  • Check caption accuracy, especially for Chinese or mixed-language speech.
  • The free plan has limited credits and features. Check the current plan.

Pricing

Free plan with limited credits; paid plans for more. Plans change, so check the official pricing page.

Quick start

  1. Sign up and paste a video link or upload a file.
  2. Let it generate clips, then review the suggestions.
  3. Edit captions and trims, export, and schedule the clips.
