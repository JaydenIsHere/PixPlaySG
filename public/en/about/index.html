PixPlay Learning

About

There are too many AI tools and too much hype. We explain each one neutrally: what it does, who it suits, its limits, and how to start, so you can decide for yourself.

What this is

PixPlay Learning is a bilingual place to understand the AI and software tools people talk about: what each one does, who it suits and how to use it at work or for content. We also share step-by-step guides on content, automation and affiliate marketing.

Why it exists

Search "passive income" and you'll find big promises and expensive courses. What's harder to find is a plain explanation of how it actually works, what it costs, which tools you need, and how long it really takes. That's what we write.

Who's behind it

PixPlaySG, a content studio based in Singapore. We make content for businesses, and we build our own AI tools, like the Strategic Script System. Everything we teach here is something we use in our own work.

How we pick tools

  • We only mark a tool "We use this" if we really use it.
  • We say who a tool is not for, too.
  • Comparisons are neutral. We show the trade-offs and let you decide.
  • When a link earns us a commission, we say so.

What you won't find here

  • Income promises or "quit your job in 90 days".
  • Fake reviews, fake screenshots or made-up success stories.
  • Tools we haven't tried, dressed up as recommendations.
