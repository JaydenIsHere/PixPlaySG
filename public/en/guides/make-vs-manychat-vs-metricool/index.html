Make vs ManyChat vs Metricool: which one do you need first?

7 min read

Three popular tools that people often mix up. What each one actually does, the pros and cons, and a simple way to decide which to set up first.

These three come up whenever people talk about "automating" their content, and beginners often think they do the same thing. They don't. Each one solves a different problem, so the right first tool depends on where your time goes now.

We use all three in our own work. Here's a neutral side-by-side.

Free plans and limits change, so check each tool's pricing page before you plan around them.

Metricool: schedule and measure

  • Good: one calendar for Threads, Instagram, TikTok and more; suggested best times; stats in the same place.
  • Not so good: the free plan caps how much you can schedule each month; it won't reply to people for you.
  • Pick it if: you post by hand every day and want to batch the week in one sitting.

ManyChat: replies that answer themselves

  • Good: someone comments a keyword and gets your link in their DMs automatically; great for free downloads and FAQs.
  • Not so good: it doesn't work on Threads; the useful features are mostly on the paid plan; you need to write the replies well or they feel robotic.
  • Pick it if: you're active on Instagram and answer the same DMs again and again.

Make: connect everything

  • Good: very flexible. A new form entry can go to a sheet, an email and a message, all by itself. Works with thousands of apps.
  • Not so good: takes the longest to learn; easy to build something more complicated than you need.
  • Pick it if: you copy and paste the same information between apps every week.
