Don't lose your account: how to stay within Threads and Instagram's rules

7 min read

Why accounts get suspended when you automate or post links, the posting habits that keep you in good standing, how to secure your account, and what to do if it happens anyway.

Automation and links are how you grow online. They're also the two things platforms watch most closely for spam. Accounts usually get restricted not because of one post, but because of a pattern. Here's how to keep yours healthy.

⚠️
No trick guarantees an account will never be restricted, and platforms change their systems often. What works long term is simple: post like a real person who's genuinely helping, and follow the rules.

What usually leads to a suspension

  • Too much, too fast. A brand-new account posting many times a day from day one looks like a spam account, even if the content is good.
  • A link in almost every post. Posts that only exist to send people elsewhere get treated as spam, especially links to free shared web addresses that spammers also use.
  • Nobody home. Accounts that publish automatically but never reply or talk to anyone look like bots.
  • The same content on several accounts. Copy-pasting posts across accounts you own.
  • Hidden ads. Affiliate or paid posts with no disclosure.
  • Weak security. A hacked account that posts spam gets suspended too.

Safer posting habits

HabitSafer approach
Posts per day2–3 for an established account. Start new accounts at 1–2 a day for the first few weeks.
LinksIn at most 1 of every 3 posts. Let the rest stand on their own, or point to the link in your bio.
Replies per postKeep threads short: a post plus 1–3 replies is plenty.
Where links goYour own website address, rather than free shared ones.
Being present10 minutes a day replying to comments and joining other people's conversations yourself.
DisclosureMark affiliate links and paid partnerships clearly.
💡
Use scheduling tools that connect through the platform's official system, like Metricool. Avoid apps that ask for your password or auto-follow, auto-like or mass-message people. Those break most platforms' rules.

Secure your account today

✅ Checklist

If your account does get suspended

  1. Don't panic, and don't keep trying to log in again and again.
  2. Find the platform's email or in-app notice and use its official review or appeal option. Keep your message short and honest.
  3. Be patient. Reviews can take days.
  4. Don't create a new account to replace it while you wait. Most platforms treat that as getting around a suspension, and it can affect your other accounts.
  5. Ignore anyone offering to "recover your account for a fee". These are almost always scams.

Don't build on one platform only

Even careful accounts can be caught by mistake. That's why it pays to own part of your audience: a simple website, an email list, and a second platform. If one door closes, the others stay open.
