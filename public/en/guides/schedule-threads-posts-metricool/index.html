Step 1 · Create your account and brand

Sign up, then create a brand. A brand is one business or one personal account, with all its social profiles grouped together.

Step 2 · Connect Threads

In your brand's settings, choose Connect next to Threads and log in with the account you post from. Connect Instagram too if you use it. You can post to both from the same calendar.

💡
Use the same phone or browser where you're already logged in to Threads. It makes the connection screen much quicker.

Step 3 · Load your 7 posts

  1. Open the planning calendar.
  2. Click a day to create a post, and tick Threads as the network.
  3. Paste your text (under 500 characters) and add an image if you have one.
  4. Pick the date and time, then schedule it.
  5. Repeat for the other 6. It takes about 10 minutes once your posts are written.

Don't have 7 posts yet? Write them first with the 3-prompt routine.

Step 4 · Pick better times

Metricool shows suggested best times to post, based on when your audience is usually online. Start with those, and keep your times roughly the same every day so followers know when to expect you.

Step 5 · Spend your saved time on replies

The first hour after a post goes out matters. Reply to every comment yourself, because conversations help a post reach more people. This part shouldn't be automated.
